Decision rules:
- A major decision is one that a human reviewer would want to approve. Examples: scope, an acceptance criterion that you added or changed, a public interface, a data shape, a new dependency, an architecture choice, a trade-off between correctness, speed, and simplicity.
- Choose the option that is easiest to reverse when two options are about equal.
- Record each major decision in the `decisions` array of your output. Give the context, the decision, each option that you did not take with the reason, the trade-offs, the reason, and how to change it.
- Do not record small implementation choices, such as a variable name or the order of statements.
